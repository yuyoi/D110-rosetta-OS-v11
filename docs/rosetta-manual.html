1 About Rosetta

Rosetta started on the Roland U-110: put your own sounds where the factory sounds were. Now it does the D-110 too. A D-10 version is on the way.

Rosetta ROMYour own sounds in the synth.
Rosetta OSNew stuff to play with. This manual.

2 Before you start

Install noteTwo chips. The OS chip is the same since v8, so an update only swaps the control chip. v12 is tested in the simulator only. v9 is the last version tested on a real unit.

Turn it on. You see the Rosetta screen, then the normal screen. Everything Roland made still works. Your Rosetta settings stay saved when you turn it off.

3 Quick screen & knobs

Hold Enter, press Edit.

Cut 64 Res 12P1 Atk 20 Rel 45
ButtonDoes
Group ▲▼Brighter / darker (cutoff). You hear it right away.
Bank ▲▼More / less ring (resonance). You hear it right away.
Number ▲▼Slower / faster start (attack). Next note.
Part ▲▼Longer / shorter end (release). Next note.
PartNext part, P1–P8
EditRosetta menu
ExitBack

Knobs over MIDI: CC74 cutoff, CC71 resonance, CC73 attack, CC72 release.

Cutoff and resonance only work on synth sounds, not samples (PCM).

10 Info & help

Info Mem oooo P01 v12

o = memory OK. X = problem. Then the part and the version.

ProblemFix
Screen says “D-110 ROSETTA”, menu does nothingControl chip not found. Check the install.
Notes keep playingExit and pick the patch again.
Cutoff does nothingIt's a sample sound. No filter on samples.
Lab went silentLab Off.
Settings gone after updateSet them again.
Tempo a bit offUse MIDI clock.
